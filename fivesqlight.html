About

Small on purpose.

Good content isn't about volume. It's about knowing exactly what you're trying to say, and saying it well.

I run Fivesquare as a boutique studio so every project gets my direct attention, not a rotating account team. Over the past decade I've worked with global brands, not-for-profits and owner-run businesses. All of them had real expertise and real assets. Few had a system to turn that into content that works.

So the job isn't only cutting footage. It's working out why the content isn't landing, fixing it upstream, and leaving you with something your team can keep running.
