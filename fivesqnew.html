01How it works

Filmed where you are. Finished overnight.

Fivesquare is based in Bengaluru, a few hours behind Sydney. The edit carries on after your working day ends, and the process runs the way a good agency's does: everything agreed in writing, dated, and reported on.

  1. 01

    Plan

    We agree the brief, shot list and script structure in writing before anyone presses record.

    You getProposal, signed scope, onboarding pack

  2. 02

    Shoot local

    Your team or a local crew we brief does the filming. We direct live on a video call so nothing gets missed.

    You getShot list on the day, live direction

  3. 03

    Finish

    Edit, colour, sound and every cut-down you need, on dates you can plan around.

    You getMaster film plus web, social and sales cuts

  4. 04

    One page each month on what shipped, what it did and what comes next.

What a normal turnaround looks like.

Send footage before close of business and the first cut is waiting the next morning. Changes go back the same way, so a round of edits takes a day instead of a week.

06About

Small on purpose.

Good content comes from knowing exactly what you're trying to say, and saying it well.

I run Fivesquare as a boutique studio so I'm directly involved in every project, from the first call to the final export. Over the past decade I've worked with global brands, not-for-profits and owner-run businesses. Most had real expertise and good footage. Few had a system for turning it into content that works.

So alongside the edit, I look at why the content isn't landing, fix it before the next shoot, and leave your team with a process they can keep running.
