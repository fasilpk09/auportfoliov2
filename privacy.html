Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Who we are

Fivesquare is a film production and post-production studio run by Muhammed Fasil from HSR Layout, Bengaluru, India, working with businesses in Australia and New Zealand. In this policy, "we" means Fivesquare. For anything about your information, email hello@fivesquare.co.

What we collect

  • When you send an enquiry: your name, email, business name, the service you're interested in and anything you write in the message.
  • When you book a call: the details you give our booking service, Calendly.
  • When you email us: whatever the email contains.
  • When we work together: contact details for you and your team, project files and footage you share, and the billing details we need to invoice you.

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Services this site relies on

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads.
  • Film thumbnails load from YouTube's image servers. A video only loads when you press play, from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • Links to Vimeo, YouTube and our partners take you to their sites, which have their own policies.
  • Enquiries reach us by email, either through your own email app or a form delivery service.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, prepare a proposal, deliver and report on the work, invoice, and keep the records the law requires. We don't sell it, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

We only share your details with our partners (Growthfather, Credot or Pelago) when you've asked us to bring them into a project, and with specialists working on your project only as far as the work needs.

Footage and project files

We keep project material for the period set out in your project scope, or until you ask us to delete it, apart from anything we must keep for legal or tax reasons. We don't use your footage in our showreel or on this site without your permission.

Where it's stored

Our team works from India, and the cloud services we use may store data in other countries. We use reputable providers, limit access to the people doing the work, and take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your choices

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Email hello@fivesquare.co and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're in Australia or New Zealand and unhappy with how we've handled it, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
